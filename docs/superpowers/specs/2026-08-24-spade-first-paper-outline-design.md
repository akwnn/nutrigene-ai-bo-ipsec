# Design Specification: SPADE-First Paper Outline

## Objective

The existing project-understanding outline will be rewritten as a publication-grade, SPADE-first manuscript blueprint. The revised document must make the design-space problem, the SPADE method, and the prospective SPADE evaluation the central scientific contribution. The BO-versus-RSM terminal-rule benchmark remains important, but it will serve as the empirical foundation for why single-recipe optimization is an incomplete laboratory objective rather than competing with SPADE for the paper's headline.

The finished document must prepare a scientific writer to draft the complete paper without reconstructing the project from code or historical experiment notes. It must therefore contain the intended argument, manuscript structure, methods, numerical results, proposed figures, statistical interpretation, limitations, claims, citations, and evidence provenance in polished scientific prose.

## Central Scientific Argument

The paper will begin from the laboratory distinction between selecting one formulation and establishing an acceptable formulation region. Conventional optimizer comparisons usually score the final nominated point. The repository's point-optimization experiments show that this score is unstable to the terminal decision rule: the same campaigns can favor DoE, favor BO, or become indistinguishable depending on whether the final condition is selected by a noisy observation, an unconstrained model optimum, an in-region recommendation, or confirmation. This establishes the need for a different objective when the laboratory deliverable is a reliable operating region.

SPADE will then be presented as a two-round, 48-well spread-and-refine strategy for learning that operating region. The central positive result is not that SPADE universally beats every optimizer. It is that SPADE produces strong design-space maps with substantially fewer experimental decision rounds, while retaining practical parity on point regret in the registered target condition. In the target Hill condition, the three SPADE variants occupy the leading map range at 0.1770–0.1804 symmetric-difference error; `spade_cf_m4` is numerically lowest at 0.1770, while the registered primary `spade_cf_m0` scores 0.1804 and outperforms all BO, classical, and one-shot space-filling comparators. qLogNEI achieves the lowest Rule-P regret at 0.0691, while `spade_cf_m0` remains within the 0.02 practical-equivalence margin at 0.0844. SPADE requires two rounds rather than qLogNEI's ten.

The paper will distinguish this demonstrated method-level performance from narrower causal and certification claims. Boundary-targeted plate-two placement does not outperform an equal-well random second plate, so the targeting mechanism is not established. The second plate improves over the 40-well plate-one-only reference by 0.0117145 in symmetric-difference error, but the gain is below the prespecified 0.02 smallest effect of interest. Local allocation with positive `m` does not satisfy the registered regret-and-calibration conjunction. Certificate evidence is valid on the constructed Hill family at sufficient Monte Carlo resolution but is not established generally: Levy and Rosenbrock under-cover at the highest confidence level, while Ackley and Hartmann6 generally decline to certify. These results narrow the mechanistic and guarantee claims without negating SPADE's mapping performance.

## Manuscript Architecture

The document will be organized as a near-manuscript blueprint rather than a collection of notes. It will open with a proposed title, a structured abstract in complete prose, and a concise statement of significance. The Introduction will move from the biological cost of multicomponent formulation experiments to the distinction between a best recipe and a design space, then explain the limitations of published BO-versus-DoE comparisons and introduce SPADE as the response to that gap.

The Methods will describe the Hall-inspired but fully synthetic biological scenario, the Hill landscape generator, external benchmark families, observation model, matched well and round budgets, BO and classical comparators, terminal rules, design-space thresholds, SPADE's two plate stages, cross-fitting, calibration and containment metrics, prospective registration, pairing, inferential units, and multiplicity control. Mathematical definitions will use standard Markdown math and will be accompanied by biological interpretation.

The Results will be organized around scientific claims rather than internal experiment chronology. The first section will show why point optimization does not answer the design-space question. The second will establish retrospective map reordering and diagnose the classical arm. The third will present SPADE's target-condition map, regret, and round results. The fourth will analyze the two-plate mechanism and local-allocation ablations. The fifth will present cross-family and dimensional robustness. The sixth will evaluate calibration, refinement, containment, and certificate behavior. The seventh will synthesize the prospective registered study and state exactly which claims are supported, narrowed, inconclusive, or unsupported.

The Discussion will lead with practical consequences for laboratories: choice of deliverable, need to prespecify the terminal rule, value of reducing plate cycles, and the distinction between a useful map and a calibrated guarantee. It will explain why the negative ablation results strengthen the credibility of the paper, because they isolate what SPADE achieves from the mechanism originally proposed to explain it. It will end with the requirements for prospective wet-lab validation.

## Figure and Table Program

The main paper will use four figures. Figure 1 will depict the laboratory decision problem and the two-round SPADE workflow, contrasting one-recipe optimization with operating-region estimation. Figure 2 will show that point regret and map quality reorder methods on identical campaigns, using the classical arm as the clearest arm-level reversal. Figure 3 will present the registered target-condition comparison across map error, Rule-P regret, wells, and rounds, making SPADE's map leadership, regret parity, and two-round cost visible in one coherent display. Figure 4 will present mechanism and reliability: targeted versus random plate two, plate one versus plate two, local allocation, calibration/refinement, and certificate outcomes across families.

Supplementary figures will contain the terminal-rule reversal, search-versus-identification decomposition, unconstrained quadratic saddle diagnosis, cost curves through 200 wells, per-family design-space results, empty-set rates, Monte Carlo draw sensitivity, and complete kill-ledger outcomes. Main tables will report the methods and budgets, target-condition estimates with intervals, cross-family map/regret ranks, and a claim-evidence matrix. Every figure specification will include the scientific question, panels, visual encodings, statistical annotations, draft caption, main conclusion, and explicit non-claim.

## Writing and Formatting Requirements

The rewritten outline will use complete professional prose. It will not explain the project through fragmentary bullet points, terse implementation notes, or one-line definitions. Lists will be retained only where they are genuinely the clearest form, such as a compact table of numerical outcomes or a final submission checklist. Internal identifiers such as E2, Q58, K6, or KF-3 will not interrupt the main narrative; they will appear in provenance notes or evidence tables.

The tone will match a high-quality interdisciplinary methods paper. Biological meaning will precede computational terminology. Claims will be expressed with their scope, estimand, comparator, terminal rule, and evidence category. Positive findings will be stated confidently where supported. Negative and inconclusive findings will be used to refine mechanism and generalizability rather than to imply that the entire method failed.

Equations will use `$...$` for inline math and `$$...$$` for display math. Every displayed quantity will be defined in adjacent prose. Tables will avoid embedding complex multi-line formulas. Figure captions will be written as publication-ready explanatory prose rather than placeholders.

## Evidence Hierarchy and Accuracy Controls

Committed result artifacts, not historical prose summaries, will be the numerical source of truth. The latest sections of `docs/FINDINGS-SPADE-FINAL.md` and the combined `results/final-spade-*.json` artifacts take precedence over earlier provisional sections. Where a prose interpretation conflicts with the sign convention in the analysis code or the raw arm means, the rewritten document will report the arithmetic transparently and flag the discrepancy rather than repeat the inconsistent label.

The target-condition positive claims are that the SPADE family occupies the leading map positions, that the registered primary `m0` arm outperforms every BO, classical, and one-shot space-filling comparator on map error, that it achieves practical Rule-P regret parity with qLogNEI, and that it uses one fifth as many decision rounds as qLogNEI at equal wells. The main negative claim is that boundary-targeted plate-two selection did not beat equal-well random placement. The plate-one comparison must be described as a statistically detectable but sub-SESOI improvement, not as an effect in the wrong direction. Certificate claims must distinguish under-coverage from declining to certify.

The synthetic Hill benchmark will be described as biologically inspired but not fitted to endothelial data. The observation noise will be described as a benchmark condition rather than a realistic biological coefficient of variation. Algorithmic seeds will not be called biological replicates. The point-optimization and prospective design-space studies will be identified as separate evidence bodies and then connected through the paper's scientific argument.

## Completion Criteria

The rewrite is complete when a reader can understand why SPADE was created, how both plates are generated, how its design space is scored, which comparators were run, what the registered target result was, how the result generalized, which mechanistic hypotheses failed, what the certificate does and does not establish, and how to convert the outline directly into a manuscript.

The final document must include a title and abstract, full Introduction/Methods/Results/Discussion prose, a four-figure main storyline with captions, supplementary figure and table plans, exact central numerical results, a literature-purpose map, strongest claims and prohibited overclaims, reviewer-risk responses, wet-lab validation design, artifact provenance, and a publication-readiness checklist. All Markdown and math must pass structural verification, and every quoted central number must be cross-checked against a committed artifact.
