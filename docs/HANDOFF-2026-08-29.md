## 5. Conventions that must not be broken

- **`results/*` is gitignored.** New result files need an explicit `!results/name.json` in
  `.gitignore`. This has silently swallowed results three times in this project's history,
  including once by me.
- **Write the analyser and freeze the gate BEFORE the data lands.** Every spec here does.
- **Read `ce_empirical_*` for truth containment, never `ce_contain_*`** — the latter is the
  circular internal statistic and sits at 1.0000 regardless of whether the certificate is
  right.
- **No co-author trailers in commits.**
