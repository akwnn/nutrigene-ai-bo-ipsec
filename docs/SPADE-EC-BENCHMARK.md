# EC differentiation benchmark

This registered synthetic benchmark compares SPADE with matched 48-well screened and unscreened DOE/RSM comparators in six formulation/process factors. SPADE and the qLogNEI control use a 32-well opening and two adaptive 8-well batches (three decision rounds). The classical DOE comparators retain their existing exact 48-well protocols, including centre replicates where their response-surface designs require them; their recorded stage counts are not treated as a tuning axis. Point regret and joint-CQA answer validity are scored against noiseless synthetic truth only after each campaign has selected its point. `joint_success` requires every CQA threshold. The benchmark is computational evidence only, not biological efficacy or wet-lab transfer evidence.
