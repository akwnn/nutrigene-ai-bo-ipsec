## 3. The one thing to internalise

**A Clopper–Pearson lower bound at 0.90 needs >= 29 answered cells even with PERFECT
containment.** Three separate "X cannot certify" conclusions in this project were drawn
below that threshold, and all three were wrong — every certificate involved was correct,
there were just too few. See `SPADE-CONCLUSIONS-2026-08-29.md` §3.

**Standing rules now in the specs:**
- No "X cannot certify" without reporting `answered` and `contained` beside it.
- At n <= 25 seeds an effect in this project is not reliable; three significant effects
  vanished going from 25 to 32 seeds.
